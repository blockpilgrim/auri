
**Design Concept: "The Fusion Core"**

I want an interactive 3D fidget spinner that feels like a piece of bleeding-edge clean energy technology, aesthetically inspired by the Arc Reactor (Iron Man).

- **Visual Language:** Industrial High-Tech. Think brushed palladium, machined steel, exposed copper coils, and transparent energetic plasma. The object should feel like a functional power plant in the palm of your hand.
    
- **The "Impossible" Physics:** Since this is digital, the spinner should utilize magnetic levitation. The outer rings should float around the central core without physical axles. The center shouldn't be solid plastic—it should be a contained field of pure, glowing energy.
    
- **Atmosphere:** The defining characteristic of this object is **LIGHT**. It must pulse, radiate, and cast volumetric glow (Bloom effects). It should feel "alive" and dangerous if not contained.
    

**Constraint:** Do not strictly copy a specific movie prop (like the Mark I or Mark VI). Instead, procedurally generate a unique "Stark-esque" geometry that maximizes the interplay between moving mechanical parts and emitting light.
